Security at the Core

Built on the Foundations of Cybersecurity

Effective cybersecurity is not a single product, but a dynamic discipline that combines people, policies, and technology to comprehensively protect systems, networks, and sensitive data against evolving threats.

Three Pillars of Cybersecurity

01

People

Users who understand and follow secure practices. The human element is often the first line of defense.

02

Policies

Structured procedures for preventing, managing, and responding to security incidents effectively.

03

Technology

Security tools and systems used to protect devices, networks, applications, and data from attacks.

Cloud & Data

Cloud environments, data centers, applications, and stored information.

Security Principles

The CIA Triad forms the core foundation of information security.

Confidentiality

Prevent unauthorized access to information. Ensuring that data is accessible only to those with the proper authorization and clearance.

Integrity

Maintain the accuracy and consistency of information over its lifecycle. Preventing unauthorized alteration, modification, or deletion of data.

Availability

Ensure systems and information remain accessible to authorized users when needed. Defending against disruptions, downtime, and denial-of-service.
