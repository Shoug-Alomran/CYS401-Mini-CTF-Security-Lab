Learning by Capturing Flags

An Educational CTF Lab, Built for Practice

The Mini Capture-the-Flag (CTF) Security Lab Platform turns cybersecurity theory into something you do. Each challenge presents a realistic scenario, hides a flag inside it, and explains the lesson once you have captured it.

About the Platform

A CYS401 Group Project

The CTF Security Lab is our semester project for CYS401: Fundamentals of Cybersecurity. This site presents the four challenge tracks, shows how flag submission and progress tracking work, and documents how the platform was built.

Anatomy of a Challenge

01

The Scenario

A short, realistic situation to work through, with a difficulty rating, a point value, and a hint if you need one.

02

The Flag

A hidden string in the form CYS401{...}. Finding it proves you solved the task; submitting it scores the points.

03

The Explanation

Educational feedback shown after a correct submission, covering why the flaw existed and how it is prevented.

What the Platform Does

Challenges & Hints

Twelve challenges across four categories, each with a scenario and an optional hint.

Flag Validation

Submitted flags are checked and answered immediately, right or wrong.

Progress & Scoreboard

Solved challenges, points earned, and completion status are tracked as you go.

Challenge Categories

Twelve challenges, four tracks, 1,200 points in total.

Web Security

Injection, broken authentication, and exposed data in a deliberately vulnerable web application.

4 challenges · 350 pts

Cryptography

Encoding mistaken for encryption, classical ciphers, and keys handled badly on purpose.

3 challenges · 300 pts

Security Awareness

Phishing emails, weak passwords, and social engineering you have to spot before you fall for them.

3 challenges · 250 pts

Cybersecurity Ethics

Authorization and scope, responsible disclosure, and the legal limits of security testing.

2 challenges · 300 pts
