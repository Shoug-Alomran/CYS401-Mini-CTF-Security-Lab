Decode, Decrypt, and Find Where Crypto Fails

Encoding versus encryption, classical ciphers, and why key handling matters more than the algorithm. Learn where cryptography fails in practice.

What You’ll Practice

Each challenge targets a different misunderstanding about what cryptography does and what it does not.

01

Encoding vs Encryption

Base64 and hex hide nothing. Recognising an encoding for what it is comes before any real analysis.

02

Classical Ciphers

Substitution and shift ciphers that fall to frequency analysis or a few minutes of brute force.

03

Key Handling

Reused keys, short keys, and unsalted hashes — strong algorithms undone by how they were used.

What You Take Away

Cryptographic failures in practice are almost never broken maths — they are broken usage.

  • Know what each tool provides

    Encryption gives confidentiality, hashing gives integrity, signatures give authenticity. They are not interchangeable.

  • Never roll your own

    Use reviewed libraries and current standards such as AES-GCM and SHA-256 rather than a scheme you invented.

  • Treat keys as the secret

    The algorithm is public by design. All the protection lives in the key, so how it is generated, stored, and rotated is the security.

  • Salt and stretch passwords

    Unsalted fast hashes fall to rainbow tables. Password storage needs a slow, salted function such as bcrypt or Argon2.
