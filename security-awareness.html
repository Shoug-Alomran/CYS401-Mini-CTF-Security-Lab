Workshop 03 · Security Awareness

Spot the Trap Before You Click

Phishing, weak passwords, and social engineering: the human side of security. Learn the warning signs attackers rely on you to miss.

What You’ll Practice

The human attack surface is the one no firewall covers. Recognising the pattern is the whole defence.

01

Phishing Recognition

Sender addresses, urgency, mismatched links, and the small details that separate a real message from a forged one.

02

Password Hygiene

Why length beats complexity, what reuse costs, and how quickly a weak password falls to a wordlist.

03

Social Engineering

Pretexting, baiting, and tailgating — manipulation that never touches a technical control.

What You Take Away

Awareness is a habit rather than a tool, and it is the control that scales to every employee.

  • Verify through another channel

    If a request is unexpected, confirm it using contact details you already had, never the ones in the message.

  • Urgency is the warning sign

    Pressure to act immediately is the most reliable indicator of a social engineering attempt.

  • Use a manager and MFA

    Unique long passwords plus a second factor mean one stolen credential does not open everything.

  • Report, do not just delete

    Reporting a phishing attempt protects colleagues who received the same message.
