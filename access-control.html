Identity & Access

Control Who Gets Access — and What They Can Do

Access control establishes identity, verifies users, determines their permissions, and records their activities to maintain accountability.

The Access Control Flow

  1. 01

    Identification

    Who are you claiming to be?

    Claiming an identity when attempting to access a secured area or system.

  2. 02

    Authentication

    Can you prove it?

    Proving that you are the claimed identity through credentials or factors.

  3. 03

    Authorization

    What are you allowed to do?

    Defining which resources and objects a specific identity is permitted access to.

  4. 04

    Auditing

    What did you do?

    Recording logs of events and activities related to the system and its subjects.

  5. 05

    Accountability

    Can it be traced to you?

    Reviewing logs for compliance so subjects can be held responsible for actions.
