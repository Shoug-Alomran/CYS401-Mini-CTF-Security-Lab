How the CTF Lab Works

Every challenge follows the same loop: read the scenario, work out the answer, then submit the flag you captured.

1. Start a Challenge

Pick a category and open a challenge. Each one comes with a scenario, a difficulty, a point value, and hints if you get stuck.

2. Capture the Flag

Solve the task to uncover a hidden flag — a short string in the form CYS401{...} that proves you worked it out.

3. Submit & Learn

Submit the flag for validation. Correct answers score points and unlock an explanation of what the challenge was teaching.
