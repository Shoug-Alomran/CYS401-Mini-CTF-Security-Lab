Core Operational Matrix

A unified triad of capabilities that every resilient security program is built on.

Protect

Prevent unauthorized access and reduce attack surfaces with layered controls, strong authentication, and hardened systems.

Detect

Identify suspicious behavior early through auditing, monitoring, and log review before an incident spreads.

Respond

Contain threats, restore affected systems from backups, and preserve evidence so the organization can recover and learn.
