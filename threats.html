Threat Landscape

Understand What You’re Defending Against

Cyber threats exploit vulnerabilities across people, systems, networks, and technology. Understanding how threats operate is the first step toward reducing risk.

From Weakness to Attack

Risk is realized when a threat agent exploits a vulnerability. Understanding this chain is essential for defensive strategy.

  1. Vulnerability

    A weakness that could be exploited.

  2. Threat

    Circumstances with potential for harm.

  3. Attack

    An attempt to exploit a vulnerability.

  4. Impact

    The resulting damage or consequence.

  5. Control

    Protective action to reduce risk.

The Risk Equation: Threat×Vulnerability=Risk

Modern Threat Landscape

New technologies create new attack surfaces. Today’s organizations must defend against evolving vector types.

Supply Chain

Supply Chain & Third-Party

Compromised vendors, insecure SaaS providers, or malicious software updates targeting the upstream ecosystem.

Emerging Tech

AI-Enabled Threats

AI-generated phishing, sophisticated deepfakes, and automated exploit discovery powered by large language models.

Infrastructure

Cloud & API Attacks

Exploitation of cloud misconfigurations, insecure APIs, and token theft to bypass traditional perimeter security.

Internal

Insider Threats

Risks from individuals with legitimate access, including intentional malicious acts or unintentional negligence.

Convergence

Hybrid Threats

Coordinated attacks combining cyber techniques with physical security breaches or psychological operations.
